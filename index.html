Hello, I'm Jacob Prince

I am a graduate student at the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor, pursuing an M.S.E. in Electrical and Computer Engineering with a concentration in Robotics, building on my B.S.E in Computer Science Engineering and Mathematics minor. My professional passion lies at the intersection of robust software systems and physical automation.

I am deeply interested in autonomous systems, embedded software, and real-time machine diagnostics.

Outside of the lab and classroom, I enjoy tinkering with personal microcontrollers, building custom PC hardware, and keeping up with the latest advancements in artificial intelligence. Feel free to explore my resume and project portfolio to see what I've been working on.
